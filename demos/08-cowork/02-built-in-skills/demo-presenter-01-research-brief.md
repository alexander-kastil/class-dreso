# Present: Produce a Competitive Market Brief

**Guide:** [Produce a Competitive Market Brief](demo-01-research-brief.md) · runs in [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork), keep the **Workspace** panel open · each beat links the step holding its prompt

**Initial prompt** (attach `internal-positioning.docx` first):

```text
Do deep research on the European outdoor apparel and gear market for the coming season. I want the three or four trends shaping premium outdoor brands right now: sustainability claims and lifecycle transparency, the growth of trail running, direct-to-consumer versus wholesale, and repairability and circular programmes. Cite where each finding comes from and note anything a mid-premium Nordic brand should be worried about.
```

### Beat 1: Deep Research runs in the background (1 min)

**Navigate:** [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) > **New task** > **+** > **Upload images and files** · [internal-positioning.docx](demo-01-research-brief/internal-positioning.docx) · **Workspace** > **Plugins** · [Step 1](demo-01-research-brief.md#step-1-research-the-market)
**What is there:** about four minutes of **Working on 1 background task**; **Deep Research** and **Web Search** under **Plugins**; four trends with sources and a **Sources** button.

### Beat 2: Research held against your positioning (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Workspace** > **References** · [Step 2](demo-01-research-brief.md#step-2-hold-the-research-against-your-own-positioning)
**What is there:** a **Where Aurora is already aligned** table and a **Where Aurora is exposed** list quoting the three admitted gaps.

### Beat 3: The Word skill writes the brief (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Workspace** > **Output** and **Plugins** · [Step 3](demo-01-research-brief.md#step-3-write-the-brief)
**What is there:** after about six minutes `AW26 Market Brief - Aurora Outdoor.docx` under **Output**; **Word** joins **Plugins**.

### Beat 4: One task saves the brief and exports the PDF (1 min)

**Navigate:** the PDF preview on the right, then **Output** · [Step 4](demo-01-research-brief.md#step-4-turn-insight-into-a-recommendation)
**What is there:** the background task **Add recommendations and export PDF**; **5. Recommendations** with a starred **Top pick**; PDF and `.docx` under **Output**, no PDF entry under **Plugins**.
