# Present: Turn a Partner Meeting into a Follow-Up Package

**Guide:** [Turn a Partner Meeting into a Follow-Up Package](demo-01-follow-up.md) · runs in [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork), before the [meeting-prep demo](../03-custom-skills/demo-02-meeting-prep-skill.md) · each beat links the step holding its prompt

**Initial prompt** (attach both files first):

```text
Read partner-meeting-notes.docx and partnership-proposal-draft.docx. The draft was written before the call. For every item marked [OPEN] in the draft, tell me whether the meeting resolved it and what the agreed position is. Then list anything the buyer raised that the draft does not cover at all.
```

### Beat 1: The meeting answered the draft's open terms (1 min)

**Navigate:** [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) > **New task** > **+** > **Upload images and files** · [partner-meeting-notes.docx](demo-01-follow-up/partner-meeting-notes.docx) · [partnership-proposal-draft.docx](demo-01-follow-up/partnership-proposal-draft.docx) · [Step 1](demo-01-follow-up.md#step-1-reconcile-the-notes-against-the-draft)
**What is there:** each `[OPEN]` mapped to what the call settled, plus points the draft misses, such as the 41-store footprint.

### Beat 2: v1.0 is a new file, the draft stays (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Workspace** > **Output** > `partnership-proposal-v1.0.docx` · [Step 2](demo-01-follow-up.md#step-2-close-every-open-term-in-the-proposal)
**What is there:** no "OPEN" or brackets left, the draft untouched, and a **Check before sending** list.

### Beat 3: The mail is saved, not sent (1 min)

**Navigate:** the **Saved to drafts** card · [Outlook Drafts](https://outlook.office.com/mail/drafts) · [Step 3](demo-01-follow-up.md#step-3-draft-the-follow-up-email)
**What is there:** **Save draft** and **Add attachments** steps, no Send card, an empty **To** line (Markus is not in the directory).

### Beat 4: The booking waits at a card (1 min)

**Navigate:** the **Create calendar event?** card · [Outlook calendar](https://outlook.office.com/calendar) · [Step 4](demo-01-follow-up.md#step-4-book-the-follow-up-call)
**What is there:** **Medium risk**, **Pick custom time**, title, empty **Attendees**, body; **Save** gives **Approved 1 action** and **Created event**.
