# Present: Turn a Market Brief into a Repeatable Product Pitch

**Guide:** [Turn a Market Brief into a Repeatable Product Pitch](demo-01-product-pitch-skill.md) · runs in [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) and [OneDrive](https://www.office.com/launch/onedrive) · each beat links the step holding its prompt

**Initial prompt** (attach both files first):

```text
Read aw26-market-brief.docx. It ends with three prioritised moves for AW26. For each move, tell me whether it could be pitched to a wholesale partner or whether it is purely internal work, and say why. Then tell me which parts of the brief are evidence a partner would find convincing, and which parts we would never put in front of them.
```

**Navigate:** [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) > **New task** > **+** > **Upload images and files** · [aw26-market-brief.docx](demo-01-product-pitch-skill/aw26-market-brief.docx) · [pitch-deck-structure.pptx](demo-01-product-pitch-skill/pitch-deck-structure.pptx) · [Step 1](demo-01-product-pitch-skill.md#step-1-find-the-pitch-hiding-in-the-brief)
**What is there:** each of the three moves rated pitch now, pitch later or internal; the 11 percent figure flagged as unsourced.

**Navigate:** the task transcript · [evidence map](_images/pitch-evidence-map.svg) · [Step 2](demo-01-product-pitch-skill.md#step-2-map-the-house-structure-to-its-evidence)
