# Present: Compile a Q3 Business Review Deck

**Guide:** [Compile a Q3 Business Review Deck](demo-01-quarterly-review.md) · runs in [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) · each beat links the step holding its prompt

**Initial prompt** (attach the three files first):

```text
You have last quarter's review deck, this quarter's sales workbook, and my regional notes. Before you build anything, tell me how you would approach producing the Q3 review for Northern Europe: what role each file plays, what story you see in the quarter, and what you would put on the slides.
```

### Beat 1: Cowork plans before it builds (1 min)

**Navigate:** [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) > **New task** > **+** > **Upload images and files** · [q3-sales-by-region.xlsx](demo-01-quarterly-review/q3-sales-by-region.xlsx) · [q3-regional-notes.docx](demo-01-quarterly-review/q3-regional-notes.docx) · [q2-review.pptx](demo-01-quarterly-review/q2-review.pptx) · [Step 1](demo-01-quarterly-review.md#step-1-brief-the-outcome-and-read-the-plan)
**What is there:** a numbered plan, no file yet; the **Workspace** panel (right) shows **References** only.

### Beat 2: A plan is steered while it runs (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Workspace** > **Steps**, then **Output** · [Step 2](demo-01-quarterly-review.md#step-2-steer-the-plan-then-let-it-build)
**What is there:** `Aurora Northern Europe - Q3 Review.pptx` under **Output** in a PowerPoint preview: six slides, hiring cost left as a placeholder.

### Beat 3: Revisions land in the same file (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Output** > the deck > **Open in PowerPoint** · [Step 3](demo-01-quarterly-review.md#step-3-iterate-the-deck-in-place)
**What is there:** still one deck under **Output**, now with speaker notes (the preview hides them).

### Beat 4: Leaving the workspace raises a card (1 min)

**Navigate:** the **Send draft?** card in the transcript · [Outlook inbox](https://outlook.office.com/mail/inbox) · [Step 4](demo-01-quarterly-review.md#step-4-send-it-through-the-approval-gate)
**What is there:** **Medium risk**, **To**, subject, body, **Cancel** and **Send**; after **Send**, **Approved 1 action**. The attachment shows in the **Add attachments** step above the card.
