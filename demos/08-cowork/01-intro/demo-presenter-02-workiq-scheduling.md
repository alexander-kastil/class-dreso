# Present: Schedule a Meeting Where Your Week Has Room

**Guide:** [Schedule a Meeting Where Your Week Has Room](demo-02-workiq-scheduling.md) · runs in [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork), the booking is real · each beat links the step holding its prompt

**Initial prompt** (attach nothing):

```text
Without me giving you any files: how does the rest of my week look? Which days are heaviest, and where are my longest unbroken stretches of free time during working hours?
```

### Beat 1: Work IQ sees your week with nothing attached (1 min)

**Navigate:** [Cowork](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/cowork) > **New task** · [Outlook calendar](https://outlook.office.com/calendar) beside it · [Step 1](demo-02-workiq-scheduling.md#step-1-map-your-week-from-live-data)
**What is there:** a **View calendar** step (Outlook) above a day-by-day map that matches Outlook.

### Beat 2: A slot is defended, not just found (1 min)

**Navigate:** the task transcript · [Step 2](demo-02-workiq-scheduling.md#step-2-make-it-defend-the-slot-not-just-find-one)
**What is there:** ruled-out blocks with reasons (lunch, after the longest meeting, late Friday), one chosen slot, calendar unchanged.

### Beat 3: A calendar write stops at a card (1 min)

**Navigate:** the **Create calendar event?** card · [Outlook calendar](https://outlook.office.com/calendar) · [Step 3](demo-02-workiq-scheduling.md#step-3-book-it-through-the-approval-gate)
**What is there:** **Medium risk**, editable **Title**, **Attendees** and body; **Save** (leave **Always allow Create event** under its arrow off) gives **Approved 1 action** and a Teams meeting in Outlook.

### Beat 4: Work IQ briefs you from your own work (1 min)

**Navigate:** **Workspace** > **Output** · the **Update event?** card · [Step 4](demo-02-workiq-scheduling.md#step-4-brief-yourself-from-your-own-work-data)
**What is there:** `Agentic AI Prep.docx` under **Output**; the card adds it to the meeting as a **Prep document: Agentic AI Prep** link, not an attachment.
