# Lab: Create Your Autopilot

Duration: about 10 minutes, plus 10 to 30 minutes of provisioning that runs while the class continues.

## What You Build

Your own Autopilot: a name and look your coworkers will see, the starter routines you chose, and a work profile you checked.

## Prerequisites

- Your class account has a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, is in the group the admin enabled for Autopilot and is covered by a spending policy that includes Autopilot (see [Autopilot setup](../../../setup/12-autopilot-setup.md)).
- A browser. Nothing is installed on your machine.

## Steps

1. Open [copilot.cloud.microsoft](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft) and sign in with your class account.
2. On the **Home** tab, select **Create your Autopilot**.
3. Choose a name and an appearance. Coworkers see both in Microsoft Copilot, Teams and Outlook, so pick something you would be fine seeing in a team channel.
4. Review the suggested starter routines. For each one, read what it would do, then keep it or clear its check box. Keep at least one.
5. Confirm. Provisioning starts and takes 10 to 30 minutes. You can leave the page; an email and a Teams notification tell you when your Autopilot is ready. Continue with the class.
6. When it is ready, open your Autopilot by its name on the **Home** tab. Review the people, responsibilities and recurring commitments it inferred from your recent work. Correct or reset anything that is wrong, then select **Looks good**.
7. Open the management panel in the upper-right corner. Find **Activity** and your routines.
8. Open Teams and find the private one-on-one chat with your Autopilot. Send:

   ```text
   What do you know about my work this week, and where did you learn it?
   ```

   Check that every answer points to mail, meetings, chats or files you can open yourself.

## Check Your Work

- [ ] Your Autopilot's name shows on the **Home** tab.
- [ ] The profile is confirmed with **Looks good**.
- [ ] At least one routine is listed in the management panel.
- [ ] Your Autopilot answered you in Teams.

## If Something Is Off

| What you see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| No **Create your Autopilot** on Home | Your account lacks a Copilot license, isn't in the Autopilot group, isn't covered by a spending policy, or Autopilot isn't activated in the tenant. Ask the trainer. |
| You already have one | Each person can have one Autopilot. Use the one you have. |
| It stops answering during the day | The spending policy may have run out of credits. Autopilot stops without telling you. Tell the trainer. |
