# Lab: Autopilot Readiness Review

Time: 25 minutes. You review your own Autopilot the way an admin or a team lead would before trusting it with real work. No admin rights are needed.

## What You Build

A Word document `autopilot-readiness.docx` in your OneDrive with one filled-in readiness table for your Autopilot.

## Prerequisites

- Your Autopilot with the routines from [Brief, Schedule, Approve](../02-autopilot-at-work/lab-01-brief-schedule-approve.md).

## Steps

1. Create `autopilot-readiness.docx` in your OneDrive with this table:

   | Check | Finding |
   | --- | --- |
   | Owner and name | |
   | What it can reach | |
   | Routines and when they run | |
   | Boundaries in the brief | |
   | What it remembers about you | |
   | Who can talk to it | |
   | Credits indicator | |
   | What happens if you leave the company | |

2. **Owner and name.** Open your Autopilot on the **Home** tab and note its name. You are its owner.

3. **Reach.** Ask your Autopilot:

   ```text
   Which mailboxes, calendars, files and Teams chats can you work with for me, and which can't you reach?
   ```

   Note the answer. Compare it with the rule that a plugin or skill never grants access by itself.

4. **Routines.** Open the management panel and list each routine with its schedule or event.

5. **Boundaries.** Copy the boundaries line of your brief into the table. Add one boundary you now think is missing.

6. **Memory.** Ask:

   ```text
   What do you remember about me and my work?
   ```

   Find one item that is wrong or that you don't want kept, and tell your Autopilot to correct or remove it. Note what changed.

7. **Who can talk to it.** Check who in your team channel can @-mention your Autopilot, and how you would stop it in that channel.

8. **Credits.** Find the "Credits in use" indicator and note what it shows. Remember that Autopilot stops without warning when the spending policy runs out.

9. **Leaving.** Write down your answer: an Autopilot goes with its owner, so what should happen to its routines and memory when you leave? Compare your answer with how an agent with an account, which stays with the team's work, would be handled.

## Check Your Work

- [ ] Every row of the table has a finding.
- [ ] You corrected or removed one memory item.
- [ ] You added one missing boundary to your brief.
