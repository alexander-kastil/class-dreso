# Check Permissions Beyond SharePoint Site Membership

Every class account holds the licenses from [Lizenzen und Postfach](01-lizenzen-und-postfach.md) and is a **Member** of the class team site from [SharePoint Team Site](04-sharepoint-site.md). This page lists, in class order, what a lab needs on top of that. Labs that are not in the table need nothing more than the Power Platform role below.

## Power Platform Role for Every Copilot Studio Lab

Every lab from Copilot Studio Basics to Publishing & Maintaining builds in a Power Platform environment. The account needs the **Environment Maker** security role there. [create-copilot-studio-users.ps1](scripts/create-copilot-studio-users.ps1) assigns it, and in an environment the script creates it also assigns **System Administrator**, **Basic User** and **System Customizer**.
